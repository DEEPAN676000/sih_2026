About AICTE Institution Complaint Monitoring System

The AICTE Integrated Institution Complaint Monitoring & Tracking Dashboard was developed for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2026).

Historically, technical education grievances were fragmented across multiple independent bureaus: the Regulation Bureau (RB) for institutional compliance, the Public Grievance Redressal Cell (PGRC) for student and faculty grievances, and the Regional Offices (ROs) for ground inspections.

Key Architectural Breakthroughs:
  • AI-Assisted Cross-Bureau Overlap Detection: Multi-factor similarity algorithms scan AICTE Permanent IDs, concurrent bureau jurisdictions, correlated violation categories, and grievance token Jaccard text similarity.
  • 7-Role Enterprise Governance: Strict permission boundaries for RB, PGRC, Administrators, Western Regional Office (WRO), Senior Management (Council Secretariat), Colleges, and Affiliating Universities.
  • Hive AI Synthetic Image Screening: Automated evidence analysis flags digital manipulation in uploaded photographic evidence while mandating human officer review.
  • Mandatory KYC Verification: Pre-submission identity verification ensures accountable, non-frivolous complaints.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is KYC verification mandatory before filing?
A: Section 5 mandates verification to deter fraudulent or malicious petitions while protecting genuine faculty and student complainants.
Q: What happens if Hive AI flags my uploaded image?
A: AI image detection is probabilistic and does NOT automatically reject your grievance. It alerts the assigned officer to conduct forensic scrutiny.

AICTE Grievance Redressal Regulations

Official Gazette Notification Reference: F. No. 1-101/PGRC/AICTE/Regulation/2019 under the All India Council for Technical Education (Redressal of Grievance of Faculty/Students) Regulations.

Key Statutory Mandates

  • Mandatory Appointment of Ombudsman: Every affiliating university and technical institution must appoint an independent Ombudsman for student disputes.
  • Prohibition of Certificate Retention: Institutions are strictly prohibited from retaining original educational certificates of admitted students or appointed faculty.
  • 7th CPC Minimum Pay Scales: Approved technical institutions must disburse salaries via direct bank transfers compliant with AICTE 7th CPC entry pay scales.
  • Full Fee Refund: On withdrawal before the cutoff date, institutions must refund tuition fees with a maximum deduction of Rs. 1,000 as processing charges.
